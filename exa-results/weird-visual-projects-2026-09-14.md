# Weird visual projects to personalize

Exa discovery sweep, 14 September 2026. Twelve search calls requested 84 results, with duplicates and off-topic results removed during selection. Twelve selected project/repository pages were subsequently fetched. These are author-described projects, not installations or demos tested locally. Remix concepts and time estimates below are proposals, not existing features. Two-hour scopes assume a prepared web development environment and use sample data or file imports rather than new service integrations. Source availability does not establish reuse permission; check each repository's license before copying.

## 1. Orb Farm → Kristian in a Jar

Existing: a sealed aquatic ecosystem in the browser. Repository reports MIT licensing.

Personal remix: turn a week of life into a tiny aquarium. Study sessions add plants, workouts add creatures, sleep changes the lighting, unfinished ideas become sediment. Treat the mappings as playful art, not health analysis. Click a creature to reveal its originating activity. This connects to the remembered culture-plate aesthetic, without presuming the existing site needs changing.

Two-hour slice: one jar, three species, seven manually supplied days, a replay slider. Defer real integrations and realistic ecosystem modelling.

[Play](https://orb.farm) · [Source](https://github.com/MaxBittker/orb.farm)

## 2. Agent Village → The Oslo Flat Simulator

Existing: Gemini-powered pixel villagers talk, trade, vote, and form relationships. Repository available; license not verified.

Personal remix: fictional caricatures of you and your brothers share one apartment. Introduce a single espresso machine, a missing frying pan, or a kitchen-cleaning dispute. Watch speech bubbles, movement, and absurd house rules emerge. Personalities are manually authored fiction.

Two-hour slice: four characters, one room, three actions, a shared conversation, and a chaos button. A single model call per round can generate the next scene; independent agents are unnecessary for the prototype.

[Source and screenshot](https://github.com/gilhyun/agent-village)

## 3. Universe Hands → Pocket Black Hole

Existing: webcam hand tracking controls a gravitational-lensing effect and animated arm overlays; author provides a demo and videos. License not verified.

Personal remix: floating words from your project list orbit your palm. Pinch to pull them into a black hole; release to scatter them. Alternatively, draw living botanical or fictional anatomical patterns on your arms.

Two-hour slice: one gesture and one visual effect. Use keyboard/mouse fallback; defer anatomical accuracy and full hand-control vocabulary.

[Demo](https://universe-hands.vercel.app) · [Source/videos](https://github.com/Abeelha/universe-hands)

## 4. Netscape_Ghost.exe → ProcrastinationOS

Existing: a simulated browser inside a fake desktop; visual distortion and hostile buttons increase with interaction. Devpost write-up available; runnable deployment and license not verified. The write-up's text generation is partly procedural, with richer LLM text listed as future work.

Personal remix: a fictional desktop haunted by abandoned projects. Old ideas open their own windows, forgotten PDFs demand attention, and an invented 2036 version of you sends increasingly odd notifications. Clearly a contained simulation.

Two-hour slice: fake desktop, five seeded projects, one ghost personality, three escalating effects. No access to the actual operating system needed.

[Hackathon project](https://devpost.com/software/netscape_ghost-exe-chaos-browser)

## 5. Vault Kosmos → Fly Through Brain2.0

Existing: local Markdown folders and links rendered as a flyable 3D constellation. Repository reports MIT licensing; public alpha.

Personal remix: notes become star systems. Research, medicine, and creative projects form regions. A guided tour follows a path between five selected notes, with the source note available at each stop.

Two-hour slice: import twenty selected Markdown notes; map links and tags; add one camera tour. Defer whole-vault indexing and automatic semantic claims.

[Demo](https://h4r7w16.github.io/vault-kosmos/) · [Source](https://github.com/H4R7W16/vault-kosmos)

## 6. Overgrown → The Browser Is Composting

Existing: HackBeanpot 2023 project grows procedural plants as tabs accumulate. Devpost states it was published to the Chrome Store; current store availability not verified.

Personal remix: unread links become mushrooms; old research tabs develop vines; archiving one makes it release spores. Include a gentle garden-only view so the novelty remains enjoyable.

Two-hour slice: fake tab list or bookmark export plus Canvas growth. Actual extension permissions and tab control can come later.

[Hackathon project](https://devpost.com/software/overgrown-plants-for-tabs)

## 7. GitCity → Anki Metropolis

Existing: GitHub contributions become an isometric city with a driveable view. Repository available; license requires inspection.

Personal remix: days become buildings, review counts set heights, subjects become districts, and review outcomes determine window colors. Drive through a month of studying.

Two-hour slice: thirty daily totals from a file, simple buildings, orbit camera, clickable dates. Street-level driving is a stretch; live Anki collection access is unnecessary.

[Demo](https://gitcity.natrajx.in/) · [Source](https://github.com/rishabhbhartiya/GitCity)

## 8. DreamtoScene → Museum of Things That Never Happened

Existing: hackathon project describes converting dream text into a Three.js scene via structured tags, with narration. Functionality is author-reported.

Personal remix: narrate an impossible Oslo memory and get a miniature exhibit: Rikshospitalet floating over a fjord, a gym inside a tram, an apartment with an endless kitchen. Use theatrical descriptions, not claims about subconscious meaning.

Two-hour slice: three preset rooms, a handful of reusable objects, generated scene parameters and an exhibit label. Arbitrary high-quality 3D asset generation is outside scope.

[Hackathon project](https://devpost.com/software/dreamtoscene)

## 9. WigglyPaint → A Doodle Bestiary

Existing: browser drawing tool with wobbly strokes and GIF export; downloadable as standalone HTML. The creator explicitly distinguishes the original from unauthorized clones.

Personal remix: use the original to draw weird little creatures, then build a separate terrarium in which your exported drawings bounce and talk. Each one can represent a fictional project mascot or a concept you want to remember.

Two-hour slice: three manually exported creatures in a simple scene. Use the drawing tool as intended; do not present its code or interface as your own.

[Original drawing tool](https://internet-janitor.itch.io/wigglypaint/)

## 10. We Were Browsing → An Abstract Self-Portrait

Existing: Spencer Chang's artwork turns clicks, scrolling, typing, and other internet actions into visual portraits. This is artistic inspiration, not a verified reusable codebase.

Personal remix: one selected day's activity becomes an abstract print: research makes fine threads, study creates dense clusters, creative browsing adds color. Or record only gestures inside your own toy page and paint with them.

Two-hour slice: capture interaction on a single page, map gesture types to marks, export PNG. Avoid broad browser-history collection for the first demo.

[Artist's project](https://spencer.place/creation/we-were-browsing/)

## 11. Colour The Solstice → Your Own Cosmic Tree

Existing: a game-jam browser toy where gestures release rainbow creatures that become living vines on Yggdrasil. Repository reports MIT licensing and a single-file launch without a server.

Personal remix: each short voice note becomes a creature with a chosen color and motion. Over a week they assemble a strange living tree. Click one to hear or read the original note.

Two-hour slice: text input, one creature per entry, local save, generative trails. Voice capture and transcription optional.

[Source](https://github.com/aweussom/colourthesolstice)

## 12. TopoTrack → Oslo Expedition Titles

Existing: Strava activity visualization with image and animation exports. Source and author-linked deployment available; license not verified.

Personal remix: an ordinary walk becomes the opening credits of a dramatic expedition documentary. A route line crosses Oslo while captions describe mundane events with absurd gravity.

Two-hour slice: one GPX route or manually supplied path, animated line, title cards, a selected still-image export. Garmin/Strava account integration and video encoding are stretch goals.

[Demo](https://topotrack.vercel.app) · [Source](https://github.com/wiederkehr/topotrack)

## My picks

- Strongest personal art direction: Kristian in a Jar.
- Funniest group demo: The Oslo Flat Simulator.
- Fastest visual payoff if the existing demo runs well: Pocket Black Hole.
- Weirdest contained two-hour build: ProcrastinationOS.
- Most useful after the hackathon: Fly Through Brain2.0.

If retaining the earlier agent-first theme, let an agent add creatures, move objects, or create events through the same small action interface as the human controls. This is optional: several of these projects are better with simple rules than with constant model calls.
